Science-Backed Caffeine Rules for Better Sleep

90-Minute Delay Rule

Delay morning coffee by 90-120 mins after waking. Allows natural cortisol peak to clear adenosine without early afternoon crashes.

The 10-Hour Hard Stop

Stop caffeine 10 hours before bed. If bedtime is 11:00 PM, your final sip should be no later than 1:00 PM - 2:00 PM.

Protect Slow-Wave Sleep

Even if you fall asleep fine with caffeine in your system, high levels reduce restorative deep Delta sleep by up to 20%.
